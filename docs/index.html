CropKD-Net: Multi-Teacher Knowledge Distillation and Quantization-Aware Compression for Sub-150KB Cross-Crop Edge Leaf Diagnostics

Innocent Nyalala 1,2
1School of Engineering and Science, Indian Institute of Technology Madras Zanzibar, Tanzania · 2Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI (WSDSAI), Indian Institute of Technology Madras, Chennai, India
Computers and Electronics in Agriculture (Pipeline · CropGuard Series Paper 2) (Nov 2026)
Code CropGuard Series (1-6) BibTeX

TL;DR: Paper 2 of the CropGuard Research Series: distills multi-teacher CNN and Transformer plant pathology representations via Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) into an ultra-compact student network for sub-150 KB INT8 smartphone and microcontroller deployment.

Abstract

Paper 1 (CropGuardNet) revealed a key bottleneck for ultra-low-bit edge deployment in agricultural computer vision: while post-training static INT8 quantization collapses parity accuracy on hybrid SiLU-Transformer architectures due to asymmetric activation clipping, unfactorized tiny CNNs incur over 1.15 GFLOPs and slow sequential CPU execution. CropKD-Net addresses this gap through heterogeneous multi-teacher knowledge distillation paired with Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) on the leak-free 35,420-image, 8-crop, 38-class CropGuard benchmark. By aligning spatial lesion attention, feature-pyramid geometry, and crop-conditioned logit calibration during student optimization, CropKD-Net bridges high-accuracy multi-crop teachers with sub-150 KB INT8 ONNX execution for offline smallholder diagnostic devices.

Keywords: knowledge distillationquantization-aware trainingINT8 edge AIplant disease detectionmodel compressionCropGuard

Key Findings & Contributions

  1. Directly resolves the SiLU, LayerNorm, and attention clipping bottleneck under post-training static INT8 quantization identified in CropGuardNet.
  2. Employs multi-teacher logit and intermediate feature distillation over the identical UUID- and 64-bit pHash-deduplicated 35,420-image multi-crop partition.
  3. Optimizes compact depthwise student topologies with Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) for sub-150 KB INT8 ONNX deployment.
